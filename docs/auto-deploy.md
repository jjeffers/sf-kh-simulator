# Auto-Deploy Design & Implementation Principles

The `AutoDeployProcessor.gd` handles the algorithmic AI placement of ships during the deployment phase of combat. Rather than acting as a static template matcher, it operates dynamically based on physics tracing, structural rules constraints, and game scenarios.

## 1. Goal Setting & Ship Categorization

The algorithms evaluate fleet compositions to break them into 3 distinct functional roles to identify their objective during the battle.
1. **Front-line:** (`Battleship`, `Heavy Cruiser`, `Light Cruiser`, `Assault Carrier`) - Designed to take heavy fire and push into the enemy formation.
2. **Escort:** (`Destroyer`, `Frigate`, `Assault Scout`, `Fighter`) - Designed to protect flanks, screen larger ships, and intercept missiles or fighters.
3. **Support:** (`Minelayer`, `Space Station`) - Designed to operate defensively behind lines or anchor around orbital bases.

## 2. Threat Vectoring 
The center of mass of the opposing enemy fleet is calculated mathematically. Ships calculate their optimal *Facing* by locating the dot product vector pointing closest towards this average enemy centroid.

## 3. Deployment Heuristics & Scoring

Ships do not statically choose the exact same hexes every deployment. They evaluate a list of `valid_hexes` generated by the Scenario Manager and assign floating-point "scores" based on the following heuristic evaluations:
- **Orbital Gravity Bonus:** Space Stations strongly prefer (score `+2000`) being positioned structurally one hex away from a planet. 
- **Planetary Collision Avoidance:** Ships strictly avoid deploying directly on top of a planet `score -= 1000`.
- **Formation Adjacency:** Escorts are heavily rewarded (`+20`) for deploying within distance 1-2 of other previously placed friendly ships to form a dense defensive screen. Front-Line ships prefer a slightly larger buffer `distance 2-3` (`+15`) to mitigate splash damage while remaining cohesive.
- **RNG Noise Factor:** A Gaussian noise value is applied to the final calculated score of every hex (`rng.randfn(0.0, 15.0)`). This fuzzes the exact mathematical deployment locations, causing ships to scatter organically around their theoretical "perfect" hex rather than statically stacking into the exact same column formation every time.

## 4. Scenario Rule Constraints
The Scenario engine can pass specific override exceptions.
- **Battle of Ken'zah Defaults:** Fighter wings automatically dock alongside their Assault Carrier or Station.
- **Close Escort `Megasaurus` Constraint:** Valid Hex generation evaluates specifically against the `Megasaurus` to forcefully restrict its spawn to only the `y=0` center-row hex, overwriting dynamic facing towards West (3) and Speed 5.

## 5. Collision & Structural Integrity Evasion

The ultimate speed of an auto-deployed ship is determined by simulating movement across its entire path line using its starting velocity.
Speed is dynamically curtailed in a `while` loop if it violates the following physical constraints:
- **Map Boundaries:** If a ship's starting speed trajectory implies exiting the radius of the outer map ring on Turn 1, speed is reduced.
- **Direct Planet Collision:** If a ship's starting speed trajectory passes tangibly through a planet hex, speed is reduced to avoid physical destruction.
- **Gravity Turn Structural Limit Check:** Planets bend gravitational movement. If a ship's predicted movement vector requires entering a hexside directly *opposite* to a planet (meaning the gravity well will aggressively whip the ship in a sharp vector immediately), this triggers an unrecoverable "Structural Integrity Check" failure. The auto-deploy detects this catastrophic planetary gravity trajectory and reduces speed to avoid the hex natively.

## 6. Support Infrastructure: Mines
Following ship deployment, the Auto Deploy script checks the active player's ships for payloads of `Mines`.
If mines exist, the processor locates friendly defending Space Stations and expends the total mine capacity by scattering them randomly in a protective ring spanning `dist 7-9` hexes outwardly from the Station's defending planet.
